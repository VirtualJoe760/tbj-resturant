Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

⚠ Template draft generated from restaurant settings. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

The Hood Bar & Pizza ("we") runs this website and phone ordering line. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

Name, phone and email when you order, sign up or send a form; your order history; and job applications you submit. Payments are handled by Stripe — we never see or store your full card number.

Phone orders

Calls to our ordering line are answered by an automated assistant and may be recorded and transcribed to fill your order and improve service. You'll be told at the start of the call.

Email & notifications

We send receipts and order updates. Marketing emails only go out if you opt in, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

Cookies

We use essential cookies (your cart, signing in) and privacy-friendly analytics. We don't sell your data or use advertising trackers.

Who we share with

Only service providers that run the site: payments (Stripe), email (Resend), phone ordering (Retell AI), hosting (Vercel), database (Neon).

Your California rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and opt out of any sale or sharing (we don't sell it). Email us to make a request.

Mock UI

Clickable prototype of the customer app (phone) and admin (kitchen view widens to tablet).

Styled for The Hood Bar & Pizza with their Yelp menu. Colors and whole-pie prices are placeholders.

Home shows specials and upcoming events; every “Order now” goes to the menu, and tapping a special opens it ready to customize. Events are a sample schedule.

Tap OG Combo to try the modifier sheet: required Size, removable toppings (“hold the sausage”), add-ons with Light/Extra, and Left/Right halves on a whole pie. Prices are computed by one function — the same rule the real app's pricing.ts follows.

Try the 86 board: toggling an item off greys it out on the menu.

Admin → Events: add an event and it shows up on Home and Events. Admin → More → Pages & forms: edit form fields, then submit the public page (Book a band / Private events / Jobs) to see it land in Submissions.

Staff (you're Alex, a bartender): set availability, clock in/out. Admin → Schedule: tap a cell to add a shift — availability, time off and overtime warnings show live; green cells = prefers that day.
